## 4. Findings from the raw files

1. **NRT files have no `type` column** (all four NRT files, every row). We cannot keep only vegetation fire in NRT months. Per the build plan, a static-source mask built from SP history is applied to all eras so the 2 Nov 2026 seam is not artificial. Static sources are small in rows (type 2 is 1.0% of Suomi NPP, 1.1% of NOAA-20, 1.8% of NOAA-21 and 1.7% of MODIS SP rows) but a tile counts as burning on any day with one detection.
2. **Evidence the mask matters.** NOAA-21 SP has a type 2 detection near 27.666 N, 76.129 E; the NOAA-21 NRT file has untyped detections near 27.667 N, 76.130 to 76.131 E on 5 Nov 2026. Suomi NPP SP has type 2 detections near 29.909 N, 74.944 E; the MODIS NRT file has an untyped detection near 29.910 N, 74.945 E.
3. **No SP/NRT overlap.** For all three VIIRS satellites SP ends 2026-07-31 and NRT starts 2026-08-01. MODIS SP ends 2026-08-30 and NRT starts 2026-09-03, leaving 31 Aug to 2 Sep with no rows in either file. No month has both versions for the same satellite, so the NRT-vs-SP seam check cannot be run on shared months.
4. **The whole Aug to Nov 2026 stubble season is NRT**, so it has no `type` field and depends on the mask.
5. **End dates.** Suomi NPP's last row is 2026-11-02 at 08:34 UTC; the plan's 13:00 UTC final-delivery time is not confirmed by the file. NOAA-20, NOAA-21 and MODIS NRT end on 2026-11-05, the end of the requested window. No refresh for 6 to 14 Nov was made, so those days are missing.
6. **Start dates resolved.** Suomi NPP starts 2012-01-20, NOAA-20 starts 2018-04-01 (requested from that date), NOAA-21 has an SP archive from 2023-03-01 (requested from that date), MODIS starts 2000-11-01.
7. **Cleaning-code notes.** `acq_time` has leading zeros (for example `0729`) and is read as text. MODIS confidence is numeric (for example 48); VIIRS confidence is a letter (`n` seen). Rules therefore differ per sensor. MODIS `scan` reaches 4.5 in rows inspected against about 0.4 to 0.6 for VIIRS (stretched edge pixels that tile-days smooth out). Files may end with a blank line, which is skipped when counting.
8. **Control file.** 69,639 rows over 14 years with detections on 3,457 days, and 4.0% type 2. The control box (76, 31, 79.5, 33.5) is wider than the district, so the control must be clipped to the Lahul and Spiti district polygon before it is read as low-fire.

## 7. Cleaning rules

1. Read all columns as text where needed (`acq_time`), skip blank lines.
2. SP files: keep `type` 0 only (presumed vegetation fire). NRT files have no `type`.
3. VIIRS: keep nominal (`n`) and high (`h`) confidence. MODIS: keep confidence 30 or above (tunable).
4. Drop exact duplicates.
5. Static-source mask: built from SP history (type 2 locations and tiles that burn almost every day, such as kilns, power plants and flares) and applied to all eras. The threshold is set in the pipeline script that builds the mask. M6 reviews the mask on a map.
6. Clip to the state boundary polygons (a tile is in scope if its centroid is inside).

## 8. Processing steps

1. Ingest raw CSVs to `hotspots_clean.parquet` (columns include `family`, `region`, `version`, `type`, `daynight`, `utc_date`, `local_date`, `lst`).
2. Local solar time: `local_hours = UTC hours + longitude / 15`; `local_date = UTC date + floor(local_hours / 24)`; `lst = local_hours mod 24`.
3. H3 tile-days at resolutions 5, 6 and 7, plus the resolution-3 zone, written to `tile_day.parquet` and `tiles.parquet`.
4. Coverage audit (`coverage`), `nrt_sp_pairs.parquet` and the web exports.

## 10. Checks and analysis results

- `check_contract` passes on tile_day, tiles, hourly.json, drift.json and the hotspot CSVs.
- NRT vs SP, same satellite and month: no overlapping months (see finding 3). The seam check is pending and cannot be run on this data.
- Relay (M2): NOAA-20 is the relay, Provisional, mean local time 13.15 h against a 13:30 target; NOAA-21 is about 1.5 minutes further off. NOAA-20 has 8 shared seasons with Suomi NPP, NOAA-21 has 3. Rebuilding a season from NOAA-20 alone gave about 2 to 3% above Suomi NPP for 2025 (daily match 0.986) and about 3% below for held-out 2021 (daily match 0.81, under the 0.85 pass mark).
- Peak-day overshoot (M2): in Indian Punjab on the 85 busiest days of 2012 to 2019 the straight ratio is off by about 61% per day and predicts 1.47 against a limit of 1, so the index is capped at the in-scope tile count.
- Drift (M1): Terra's median local hour falls from about 10.9 to about 9.25 by 2026, Aqua's rises from about 13.3 to about 15.3, VIIRS satellites stay near 13.1 to 13.3. The VIIRS/MODIS Oct to Nov ratio for Punjab + Haryana goes from about 2.53 in 2012 to about 1.43 in 2025. Terra's Oct to Nov tile-days fall from about 1,200 in 2012 to about 16 in 2025. Orbit drift is the likely reason but it is not confirmed, so no cause is stated in the app.

## 11. Known limits

- 2026 is partial: Suomi NPP's last data is 2 Nov 2026; other NRT files stop on 5 Nov 2026.
- Jan 2012 is partial (Suomi NPP starts 20 Jan 2012).
- The newest data are near-real-time and Provisional.
- The control download exists only for Suomi NPP; the other three control downloads were not made.
- A hotspot is a thermal detection, not a confirmed fire; no hotspot does not mean no fire.
